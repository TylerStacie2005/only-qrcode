# Privacy Policy - Only QR Code

**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

## Overview

Only QR Code does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data itself. The Mac version shows no ads and makes no network requests. The free iPhone, iPad and Android versions show ads, described below.

## Data Collection

The app itself collects **nothing**. Specifically:

- No personal information
- No usage analytics
- No crash reports
- No device identifiers of our own
- No location data
- No cookies or tracking pixels

## Data Storage

All QR codes you generate are created locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.

## Advertising (iPhone, iPad and Android)

The free iPhone, iPad and Android versions show a full-screen ad now and then after you finish with a code, served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier, IP address and device details to serve and measure ads. Where required, a consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice later where the app offers it. People who bought the app before it became free see no ads. The Mac version contains no ads.

## Third-Party Services

The iPhone, iPad and Android versions integrate Google AdMob for ads, as described above. The Mac version uses no third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools.

## Internet Access

Creating and reading codes works offline. The Mac version makes no network requests. The iPhone, iPad and Android versions contact Google only to load ads.

## Children's Privacy

Only QR Code does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is for a general audience, and ads are not directed at children.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page. We do not collect data ourselves.
